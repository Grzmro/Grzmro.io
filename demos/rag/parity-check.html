Encoder parity check

The demo page can embed a free-text question in the browser, which only means anything if the browser's copy of the encoder agrees with the one the pipeline uses. This page measures that agreement instead of assuming it — it is where the numbers quoted in the demo's caveats come from.

For each of the 35 curated questions it embeds the text with Xenova/bge-small-en-v1.5 under ONNX, compares the vector against the one fastembed produced for the same question during export, and — the part that actually matters — checks whether the resulting ranking is the same one the Python retriever produced. Both quantisations are measured, since the 8-bit weights are the ones small enough to ship.
